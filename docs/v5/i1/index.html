About Squirrel Papers

The Squirrel Papers by the Research Squirrel Engineers Network are a free Open Access journal hosting publications on Zenodo and the Open Science Framework (OSF), collecting Linked Open Data (LOD), Research Software Engineering (RSE), Cultural Heritage and Geo-Sciences related research papers, working papers, blog posts, posters, slides, research data and research software.

The Squirrel Papers are an official journal accepted by the "Nationales ISSN-Zentrum für Deutschland" with ISSN 2750-560X.

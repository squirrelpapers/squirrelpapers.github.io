Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, in server log files. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the secure and stable delivery of the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no fonts or scripts from third parties.

If you choose light or dark mode with the switch in the header, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage (localStorage, entry sqp-theme). It does not leave your computer and can be removed with your browser's site data.

PDF preview from Zenodo

Entry pages can show a PDF preview. It is loaded only when you click "Show PDF preview". Your browser then connects to zenodo.org (operated by CERN) and transmits, among other things, your IP address. The legal basis is your consent given by the click (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Map

The map of event places is drawn from data on this site (Leaflet, borders from Natural Earth). Only when you switch on the OpenStreetMap background does your browser load map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom), transmitting, among other things, your IP address. The legal basis is your consent given by the click (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); switching it off stops further loading.

Search and SPARQL queries

Search and SPARQL queries run entirely in your browser. Every file they need (search index, Python runtime, graphs) comes from this site; what you type is neither transmitted nor stored.

Links

Links to DOI, Zenodo, ORCID, Wikidata and other sites lead to third-party services; their providers are responsible for any processing of data there.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

About Squirrel Papers

The Squirrel Papers by the Research Squirrel Engineers Network are a free Open Access journal hosting publications on Zenodo and the Open Science Framework (OSF), collecting Linked Open Data (LOD), Research Software Engineering (RSE), Cultural Heritage and Geo-Sciences related research papers, working papers, blog posts, posters, slides, research data and research software.

The Squirrel Papers are an official journal accepted by the "Nationales ISSN-Zentrum für Deutschland" with ISSN 2750-560X.

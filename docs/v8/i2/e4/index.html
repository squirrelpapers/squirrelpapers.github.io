Abstract

First release verified end-to-end against fdo-3d-packager's real output (CIIC 81, Freshford; fdo-3d-packager commit 7ddf1a8). Highlights since v0.3.1: correct fdo:role classification for .mtl/viewer//data/textures/ (S18), PNG+SVG-only diagram output (S19), optional creators[].id (S20), fixed auxiliary-role/CC-license/file-count/placeholder gaps in the overview diagram (S21). Known limitation: no automated tests/CI yet - verification has been per-change, against fresh clones and real production data, documented in PRIMER.md.

About Squirrel Papers

The Squirrel Papers by the Research Squirrel Engineers Network are a free Open Access journal hosting publications on Zenodo and the Open Science Framework (OSF), collecting Linked Open Data (LOD), Research Software Engineering (RSE), Cultural Heritage and Geo-Sciences related research papers, working papers, blog posts, posters, slides, research data and research software.

The Squirrel Papers are an official journal accepted by the "Nationales ISSN-Zentrum für Deutschland" with ISSN 2750-560X.
